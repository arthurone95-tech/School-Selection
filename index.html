What this prototype covers

  • A checked subset of the 2025 register: 27 general schools plus a few TVET, STEM and SEN schools. Not the full national list.
  • Uses official 2025 categories. Where a detail is missing, such as a programme code, it is left blank rather than guessed.
  • Special pathways change which schools are offered. Some pathway and Day/Boarding rules are still being confirmed, so they are marked needs verification, not enforced.
  • Your choices are saved in this browser only. Nothing is submitted.
