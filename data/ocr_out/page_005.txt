SELECTION GUIDELINES

a) Select seven (7) schools in total, five (5) main, two (2) alternative schools.

i. Your five (5) main schools should be ranked in order of preference, with the 1st being the most preferred choice.

ii. Your two (2) alternative schools should be selected from Appendix “3” for consideration if candidate is not placed in any of their five (5) main choices.
b) Select programmes and residential preferences for each choice of school (3 Boarding and 2 day).

c) Candidates may select one (1) category B school as Boarding and another category B school as Day.

d) Candidates may select one (1) category C school as Boarding and another category C school as Day.

Note that a candidate placed in any of their choices will NOT be changed.

PLEASE NOTE THAT CANDIDATES:
a) Cannot choose more than one (1) School from Category A.

b) Cannot select more than two (2) schools from Category B.

c) May select all five (5) schools from Category C (3 Boarding and 2 Day).

d) Candidates who wish to offer purely TVET Programmes must select all five (5) TVET Institutes from CATEGORY A, B and C(using same selection criteria as indicated above)
as Day or Boarding

e) Candidates interested in pursuing careers in STEM/Science fields such as engineering, medicine or astronautics etc, must select STEM/ Science subjects in all five choices
from categories A, B and C as Day or Boarding.
