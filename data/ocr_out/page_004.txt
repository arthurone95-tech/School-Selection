2025 BECE CANDIDATES SCHOOL SELECTION GUIDELINES

As part of measures to ensure a smooth placement of qualified BECE candidates, the Ghana Education Service (GES) and the Technical and Vocational Education and Training
Service (TVETS) have put in place the following arrangements for the information of parents/guardians/candidates and the general public.
